# Idiomatic Expression Analysis

## Objective
Review flat, literal, or clichéd phrasing without inventing cultural background or voice.

## Inputs
- Character and setting signals from **manuscript_analysis.md**.
- Author-supplied voice or setting guidance, when available.

## Steps
1. **Literal Phrase Sweep**
   - Flag phrases that read as template descriptions ("in a way", "as if to", "it was like").
2. **Setting-Aligned Idioms**
   - Preserve idioms already supported by the text. New idioms are proposals requiring author approval, especially when they imply region, culture, class, age, or lived experience.
3. **Voice Matching**
   - Match only traits established in the manuscript or an approved voice guide. Do not infer education, class, ethnicity, nationality, disability, or other identity from diction.
4. **Cliché Replacement**
   - Review familiar idioms in context. Offer a story-world alternative only when the source supports every implied detail and the author approves the voice-bearing change.

## Deliverables
- List of review candidates with source-compatible proposals, if any.
- Optional idiom candidates labeled `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and mapped only to source-supported voice cues.
- Approved examples where a change was warranted; no minimum example count.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Retained or proposed idioms are compatible with established setting and voice.
- Shared wording is allowed; difference is not forced for its own sake.
- No unapproved cultural, biographical, or experiential detail is added.
